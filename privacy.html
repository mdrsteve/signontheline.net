Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

This policy explains what information signontheline.net collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. Sign on the Line is a sole proprietorship operated by Steven Davis, a California Commissioned Notary Public based in Glendale, California.

Information collected through this website

The only place this website collects information is the contact form on the Contact page. When you use it, you choose to provide:

  • Your name, email address, and city (required)
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The type of document or service (optional)
  • When you need the appointment (optional)
  • Any additional details you choose to write (optional)

You may also contact me directly by phone, text, or email without using the form. In that case I receive whatever information you choose to send.

My web host automatically records standard technical information with each visit, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. This is ordinary server logging and is not used to identify or profile visitors.

How the form is delivered

The contact form is delivered to my email by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree processes the submission in order to pass it to me and handles it under its own privacy policy. It is not used for advertising, and your information is not shared with anyone else through it.

How your information is used

Only to respond to you and to arrange and provide notary services: confirming your appointment, quoting your fee, finding your location, and following up afterwards about the appointment or an invoice.

What this website does not do

  • It does not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • It does not share your information with anyone for marketing.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking of any kind.
  • No other party collects information about your activity on this site or across other websites.
  • There are no accounts, logins, or stored payment information on this site.

Do Not Track

Because this website does not track visitors or collect personally identifiable information about their online activity over time, it does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

Text messages

If you give me your phone number, I may text you about your appointment, such as confirming a time or letting you know I am on my way. These are messages I send personally, not automated or bulk messages, and there is no marketing list. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Ask me to stop at any time and I will.

How long information is kept, and how to review or remove it

Contact form messages and emails are kept in my business email while they are useful, and client contact details are kept in my own scheduling records so that I can serve repeat clients.

To review, correct, or ask me to delete the information you sent through this website, email steve@signontheline.net or call (818) 804-8137. I will confirm what I have and remove it on request, with one exception, described next.

Records I am required to keep as a Notary Public

This is separate from the website, and it is important to understand.

California law requires every notary public to keep a sequential journal of notarial acts, and to record certain details of each act in it (Gov. Code 8206). That journal is a state-mandated record. I cannot alter it, delete entries from it, or remove your entry at your request, and it must be surrendered to the county clerk when my commission ends.

California law also gives the public a limited right of access to it. Any member of the public who submits a written request naming the parties, the type of document, and the month and year of the notarization is entitled to a copy of that line item, and I am required to respond within 15 business days. My journal is maintained in dedicated electronic notary journal software under my direct and exclusive control, protected by access controls and encryption and backed up securely. I am the only person with access to it, and I do not disclose its contents for any other purpose except as required by law, such as in response to a subpoena, a court order, or a lawful request from the Secretary of State or a peace officer.

I read your identification to verify who you are, and I record only what the law requires me to record.

I do not keep copies of your documents as a routine practice. Occasionally, on a transaction handled through a title company, an estate attorney, or a similar professional, I keep a copy for a limited time in case a question comes up afterwards. Those copies are not retained long term, and they are destroyed once they are no longer needed.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging notary services and is not directed to children.
